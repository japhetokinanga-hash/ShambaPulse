Alerts

Alerts carry county and regional advisories to farmers in a specific ward. They are kept short so each one fits one SMS message, and they name the region rather than pretending to be a ward forecast.

How an alert is sent

  1. An officer creates an alert for a ward, in English and Kiswahili.
  2. The system finds farmers in that ward who have consented and subscribed.
  3. If no farmer is eligible, the alert stays a draft - it is not marked as sent.
  4. Delivery outcomes are recorded, so the officer can see the totals for that alert and filter them by ward.

Separating "submitted" from "confirmed delivered" matters: a message that was sent is not the same as a message that arrived.
