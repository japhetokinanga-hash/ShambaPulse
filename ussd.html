USSD and SMS: for a basic phone

A farmer does not need a smartphone to use ShambaPulse. The USSD menu works in English and Kiswahili, and alerts arrive as ordinary SMS messages.

The five-option menu

The menu offers five choices, in both languages:

  1. Weather forecast
  2. Market prices
  3. Advisories
  4. Subscribe to alerts
  5. Opt out of alerts

Registration happens through option 4, Subscribe to alerts: a new farmer chooses a ward and a language, then answers the Data Protection Act 2019 consent prompt. If consent is declined, nothing is stored. A farmer's home ward is the default, and further wards can be added.

Simulated SMS in a demonstration is a demo outcome, not a fallback for a failed delivery. In the live system, a failed delivery is recorded as failed, not hidden.
